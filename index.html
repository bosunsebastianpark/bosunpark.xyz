About

To me, love is simple: it's helping things grow. A mind growing the moment it understands. A plant growing toward the light. A person, a system, a friendship — anything alive becoming more of what it can be.

That's when I feel most alive — explaining something until it clicks, being close to nature and animals, or understanding a system until I feel part of it. It's all one motion, repeating at every scale: a cell in a body, a person in humanity, a living thing in the universe — each part joining and growing the whole it belongs to. Physics is just the name I found for the biggest rung of that ladder.

On the side of living things — helping them connect, belong, and grow.

Research

Reading the order in living and physical systems.

  • Turbulence spreading & coherent structure in plasmas Vortex and front dynamics with Prof. P. Diamond — how a coherent structure controls the spreading of turbulence (Dedalus simulations, cell-scale scans). Paper in progress. Read more →
  • Drift-wave turbulence & zonal flows in fusion plasmas Research at the Korea Institute of Fusion Energy (KFE) — modeling drift-wave turbulence with the modified Hasegawa–Mima equation, extending toward the modified Hasegawa–Wakatani system and the zonal flows that regulate it: the turbulence that governs how well a fusion plasma stays confined.
  • Physics of memory & unconventional computing Non-Markovian open quantum systems — quantifying how a system's memory, seen as information backflow, shapes its evolution — and “memcomputing,” brain-inspired machines where memory both stores and processes information, dissolving the processor–memory divide of ordinary computers.
