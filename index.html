About

Draft — your voice goes here.
I study physics at UC San Diego, where I think about how living and physical systems hold their order against a universe that pulls everything toward disorder — how a plasma, a swarm, or a mind processes information and remembers. I believe the best science is done out of love: for the world, for the people in it, and for the strange fact that matter can wake up and ask questions about itself. I like bees, trees, hard problems, and staying on the side of living things.
